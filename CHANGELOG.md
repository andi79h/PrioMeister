# PrioMeister 1.27.0 (2026-10-30)

- Priocheck: the addon now also names who has entered prios on priomeister.de but is not in the raid. The names appear in the chat and in the details on the Raid tab. The number appears in the window after checking and on the Raid tab.
- Raid members whose names WoW has not loaded yet no longer appear as “Unknown” in the priocheck and under “In the raid without prio”. They count as soon as their name arrives.
- Plus and minus for the roll tiers on the Raid tab are swapped. Plus now raises the tier by one, e.g. from 99 to 100, minus lowers it.
