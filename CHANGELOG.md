# PrioMeister 1.31.0 (2026-11-03)

- Move entries to the current raid: if the new raid was started only after the first awards, they are still in the old raid. In the “Loot” tab, select the old raid, right-click the first award of the new raid and choose “Move from here to the current raid”. It and all later entries move over, sorted by time and with their rolls. Roll tiers, prios and the export follow.
