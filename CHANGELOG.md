# PrioMeister 1.25.0 (2026-10-26)

- The Awards tab has a “Reset” button next to the raid selection. After a confirmation it deletes all saved raids, the current one and all earlier ones. They cannot be exported from the addon afterwards. Settings, lists and roles stay.
- “Share” now sits right next to the channel selection. Only for whispers does the name field come in between.
